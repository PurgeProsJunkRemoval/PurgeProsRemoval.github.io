Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How is pricing determined?

Almost entirely by volume — how much space your items take up in the truck. We look at the job (in person, by phone, or from photos) and give you a fixed price before we start. No hourly rate, no surprise line items.

What can't you take?

We take almost everything: furniture, appliances, electronics, yard debris, and construction leftovers. We can't legally take hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, or asbestos — ask us and we'll point you to the right disposal option.

Do I need to be there?

Not for the whole job. Many customers show us what needs to go, then head back to work while we handle the rest. We just need someone available to approve the final price.

What happens to my stuff?

We sort every load — usable items go to local donation centers, recyclables get recycled, and only what's left goes to the transfer station.

How fast can you come out?

Most requests get a same-day or next-day appointment. Call before noon and we can often get you on the schedule that afternoon.
